Privacy Policy

App: StreakUp

Last updated: August 24, 2026

StreakUp ("the App") helps you track goals with daily check ins, streaks, reminders, and optional subscription features. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Goal Data Stays on Your Device

The App does not require an account. Goal titles, descriptions, dates, custom reminder text, check ins, missed days, notes, and most settings are stored only on your device using Hive and SharedPreferences. We do not upload this content to our servers or include it in analytics or advertising events.

A cached premium status is also stored on your device so the App can load quickly. The current subscription status is verified with RevenueCat when the App starts and resumes.

Third Party Services

The App uses the services below. These providers may process technical or usage information under their own privacy policies.

Google User Messaging Platform and Google AdMob

Free users may receive banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads. Google may process:

  • Device or other identifiers, including an advertising identifier when available.
  • Approximate location derived from an IP address.
  • Ad requests, impressions, interactions, rewards, and related app activity.
  • Device, operating system, app version, network, diagnostic, and performance information.

Google User Messaging Platform checks whether a privacy message is required for your region. Your choice is stored on your device, so the App does not ask on every launch. A message can appear again if Google determines that a new choice is required, your previous choice expires, or you reopen Privacy choices in Settings. The App requests ads only when Google reports that ads may be requested.

On iOS, if you allow tracking in Apple's one time permission prompt, Google may use the advertising identifier for ad personalization and measurement. If you decline, ads may still be shown without that identifier.

After a premium subscription is verified, the App does not initialize Google Mobile Ads or request ads. Firebase Analytics, described below, may still operate for premium users.

For more information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use AdMob

Google Analytics for Firebase

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand reliability, feature use, audience size, and advertising performance. Google Analytics may automatically collect:

  • A randomly generated app instance identifier that identifies one installation of the App.
  • App launches, sessions, updates, and general interactions.
  • Device model, operating system, app version, language, and general geographic region.
  • Subscription and advertising events, including whether an ad request, load, display, or reward succeeded.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.

The App may send custom event names and nonpersonal status values, such as whether an ad loaded or a consent flow succeeded. It does not send your goal titles, descriptions, notes, reminder text, name, email address, or precise location to Firebase Analytics.

Google retains Analytics data according to the retention settings for the StreakUp Analytics property and may retain aggregated reports for longer.

For more information:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics data collection

Apple App Store and Google Play Billing

Apple processes iOS subscription transactions and Google processes Android subscription transactions. Depending on your platform, Apple or Google may process purchase history, transaction status, payment information, account identifiers, device information, and fraud prevention data. StreakUp does not receive your full payment card details.

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

RevenueCat

RevenueCat manages subscription status and validates purchase receipts. RevenueCat may process:

  • Subscription product identifiers, purchase dates, expiration dates, trial status, and renewal events.
  • An anonymous app user identifier and device identifiers used to associate subscription status with an installation.
  • Device type, operating system, app version, and technical information used for subscription diagnostics and compatibility.

The App does not provide your name, email address, goal content, or store account credentials to RevenueCat.

For more information: RevenueCat Privacy Policy

How Information Is Used

  • Core functionality: To provide local goal tracking, reminders, check ins, streaks, and progress views.
  • Reliability and analytics: To measure active installations, app launches, feature performance, errors, and release quality.
  • Advertising: To show ads to eligible free users, provide optional rewarded features, limit ad frequency, and measure ad performance.
  • Subscriptions: To process purchases, validate receipts, restore access, and manage premium status.
  • Legal and security: To prevent fraud, comply with law, and protect the App and its users.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Technical, analytics, advertising, and purchase information is shared with Google, Apple, and RevenueCat only as needed for the services described above. Goal titles, descriptions, notes, check ins, and reminder text are not shared with these providers by the App.

Retention

  • Local goal data and settings remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App.
  • A privacy choice may remain on your device until it expires, is reset, is changed through Privacy choices, or the App is uninstalled.
  • Third party information is retained under Google, Apple, and RevenueCat policies and the settings applied to those services.

Security

Local information is protected by the application sandbox provided by iOS or Android. Data sent to third party services uses encrypted HTTPS connections. No security method is perfect, but the App limits collection and does not operate a server that stores your goal content.

Your Choices

  • Delete local data: Delete individual goals in the App or uninstall the App to remove its local data.
  • Privacy choices: Where Google requires a privacy options entry point, open Settings and select Privacy choices to review or change available advertising and data choices.
  • Remove ads: Purchase Premium to stop Google Mobile Ads initialization and ad requests after premium status is verified.
  • Manage subscriptions: Use Manage Subscription in the App or your Apple App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
  • Device controls: Use the privacy and advertising controls provided by iOS or Android. Uninstalling the App also removes its app instance identifier from that installation.
  • Access: Goal data is stored locally and is available directly through the App.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection for information processed by third party providers. Contact us if you need help identifying the relevant provider or control.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly ask children under 13 to provide personal information. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, contact us so we can review the situation.

International Processing

Goal data remains on your device. Google, Apple, and RevenueCat may process the technical, analytics, advertising, or subscription information described above in countries outside your place of residence, including the United States, under their respective legal safeguards and privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the App, third party services, or legal requirements. The current version and its effective date will remain available through the App and the public policy page.
