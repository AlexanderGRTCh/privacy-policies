Privacy Policy

App: StreakUp

Last updated: October 23, 2026

StreakUp ("the App") helps you track goals with daily check ins, streaks, reminders, and optional Premium purchases. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

StreakUp is operated by Alexander Kemos, trading as Ktisis Arc, in British Columbia, Canada. We are responsible for the processing described in this policy. Contact app.inquiries2026@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

Goal Data Stays in Private App Storage

Ordinary habit tracking does not require an account. Optional AI chat uses an automatic secure service identity and requires explicit consent. Goal titles, descriptions, dates, custom reminder text, check ins, missed days, notes, and most settings are stored locally in the App's private storage using Hive and SharedPreferences. We do not include this content in analytics or advertising events. Optional AI chat shares the selected context explained below.

Depending on your iOS or Android backup settings, Apple or Google may include the App's local storage in an operating system or device backup and may restore it when you reinstall the App or move to another device. Those backups are controlled through your Apple or Google account and are handled under the platform provider's policies.

A cached Premium status is also stored on your device so the App can load quickly. Current purchase entitlement status is verified with RevenueCat when the App starts or you restore purchases. Purchase status updates received while the App is open are also applied.

Optional AI Chat for Adults 18+

AI chat uses an automatic secure identity managed by Supabase, without requiring an email or password. Your messages, pet name, optional display name, current goal titles, schedules and recorded progress, up to 16 recent messages, bounded excerpts of older chat and relevant memories you enable are sent over HTTPS to StreakUp and your selected AI provider for safety checks and replies. Included conversations use Anthropic. With your own API key, choose among providers currently available in the App. Goals refresh on each send. The AI only sees context that fits. StreakUp keeps account, consent, access and usage metadata. Its backend database does not store conversation text. The selected provider may retain requests under its API policies. Avoid secrets and sensitive information. Optional memories are encrypted on this device. Eligible statements expire after 30 days, explicitly saved statements after 90 days and pending drafts after 24 hours. View, edit or delete them in Conversation memory. Your current chat is stored encrypted on this device and survives reopening the App. New chat deletes the current transcript and keeps optional memories. Turning memory off deletes saved memories and the current chat. Deleting AI data or your pet leaving also clears this chat and its memories. Editing or deleting an individual memory clears dialogue that could bring its old details back. Local deletion cannot retract requests already sent or remove provider records. A personal API key is stored securely on this device for your service identity and sent over HTTPS to StreakUp, which uses it transiently with the selected provider. API billing is separate from consumer subscriptions.

When you first set up AI chat or verify a purchase, StreakUp automatically creates a secure service identity with Supabase. No email or password is required. This identifier links consent, access and usage records, so it is pseudonymous rather than anonymous activity. Existing signed in identities are retained on upgrade. Supabase processes the service identifier, authentication records and technical connection information. For existing email accounts, it also retains the email already supplied. Consent records include the notice version, acceptance time and revision. They also include the country you select as your current place of use, so we can check AI provider availability. This is your declaration, not GPS or verified location. Update it in AI service settings when you travel or move. We do not send this country field to the AI model or use it for advertising. It is removed with your AI consent records when you delete AI data. Access, usage and safety controls are associated with your chat account. These records are personal data. No date of birth or identity document is requested. You confirm that you are at least 18. You can withdraw AI consent in AI service settings. Deleting AI data revokes access and removes its authentication, consent, profile and active access records. Minimal request, daily usage and cost metadata remains without the account link for up to 90 days, plus the next daily cleanup interval. These records do not contain conversation text or personal API keys. A limited purchase reference and original purchase and hosted eligibility dates remain to restore purchases and prevent a new Lifetime AI year after account deletion or reinstall. The deleted chat account link is removed. These limited records remain while needed to restore the purchase or enforce its original AI window, including after account deletion. Deleting AI data does not cancel a store subscription. Your free habits and pet remain available.

There is no StreakUp cloud backup or account recovery for local habits, pet history, conversations, memories or personal API keys. Uninstalling, clearing app storage or changing phones can remove these and your service identity. Device backups may restore some ordinary app data, but do not provide a supported way to recover your AI identity or keys. Restore Purchases recovers eligible store access using the same store account; it does not recover this local data or restart the Lifetime AI year. For welcome offer eligibility, Apple DeviceCheck or Google Play Integrity can provide a device token and a limited signal indicating whether this phone has used the offer. Our backend verifies the signal and records the claim status and time for your service identity. Raw proof tokens are used transiently and not stored in the trial ledger. The platform flag can survive uninstalling, factory reset and deletion of AI data; it does not restore identity, consent, purchases or API keys. We do not use this signal for advertising tracking. Contact app.inquiries2026@gmail.com for questions or applicable correction requests. A new service identity requires its own AI consent. Uninstalling does not itself request deletion of backend records. Use Delete AI data before uninstalling when you want to remove them.

These notices explain the feature and do not waive your legal rights. For privacy requests or concerns: app.inquiries2026@gmail.com.

Export chat and memories shares a readable, unencrypted JSON copy of your current chat and saved or pending memories. It excludes memory vectors and stored API credential fields. Anything you typed into the conversation may still be in the export. Protect the copy and choose where you share it. Temporary share copies may be kept by your operating system or sharing app. Exported copies are outside StreakUp deletion controls. Export is not a cloud backup or an import and restore feature. Current chats are limited to 2,000 messages or 8 MiB; at capacity the App asks you to export and start a new chat. It does not silently erase older displayed messages. The AI receives only selected context, so storing your chat does not guarantee perfect recall.

AI Providers and Supabase

Included AI chat uses the Anthropic API. Personal API connections support Anthropic, OpenAI and Google Gemini when available in AI service settings. Before conversation data is sent, the AI notice names your selected provider and asks for permission. Using a personal key changes who pays the provider; it does not make the conversation stay entirely on your phone. StreakUp routes the request through its backend for access checks, safety processing and replies. Switching providers never silently falls back to our paid AI service.

Provider retention and use of requests depend on the provider, API service, account settings and legal obligations. In particular, Google distinguishes paid and unpaid Gemini API services; unpaid services may use submitted content to improve its products and may involve human review. Do not assume that a personal key is a paid service or has zero retention. Review the provider terms before connecting it, and never submit secrets or sensitive personal information. StreakUp does not use your chat or shared goals for advertising.

  • Google Gemini API terms and data use

The in app reporting control sends a selected report category associated with your service identity so we can investigate safety and service problems. It does not attach your conversation text. Reports remain for up to 90 days plus the next daily cleanup; deleting AI data removes the identity link. Reports are not monitored in real time and are not an emergency service. If you separately email support, we receive the content and contact details you choose to send.

Retention

Optional local memories expire after 30 days, explicitly saved memories after 90 days and pending drafts after 24 hours. Account deletion clears corresponding local memories and recent chat. Unlinked cloud request and usage metadata remains for up to 90 days plus the next daily cleanup. Limited purchase references and original purchase and hosted eligibility dates remain while needed to restore the purchase or enforce its original AI window, including after account deletion. The deleted chat account link is removed.

  • Local goal data and settings remain in the App's private storage until you delete them or remove the App from the current installation.
  • Copies may remain in an operating system or device backup until that backup expires or is deleted under your Apple or Google account settings. Reinstalling the App may restore a backup.
  • A privacy choice may remain in local app storage or an operating system backup until it expires, is reset, is changed through Privacy choices, or the applicable backup is deleted.
  • Third party information is retained under Google, Apple, RevenueCat, Resend, Stripe and PayPal policies and the settings applied to those services.

Security

Local information is protected by the application sandbox provided by iOS or Android. Data sent to third party services uses encrypted HTTPS connections. No security method is perfect, but the App limits collection and does not store conversation text or shared goal content in its backend database. AI providers process requests under their own API policies and may retain them. There is no promise of zero retention.
