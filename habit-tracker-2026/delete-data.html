Delete StreakUp AI data

StreakUp is operated by Alexander Kemos, trading as Ktisis Arc. You can stop using AI and keep your local habits and pet.

In the App

  1. Open Settings, then AI service.
  2. Choose Delete AI data and read the confirmation.
  3. Confirm to delete the current AI service identity, consent, profile and active access records, and clear the saved AI key, chat and memories on this device.

Conversation memory also lets you view, edit or delete individual memories. New chat clears the current transcript while keeping optional memories. To remove an ordinary habit, delete it from your habit list. Deleting AI data does not delete your local habits or cancel a store subscription.

Request help without the App

Email app.inquiries2026@gmail.com with the subject StreakUp data deletion request. Tell us whether you still have access to the installation and what you want deleted. For a legacy email account, use its email address where possible. Do not send passwords, API keys, authentication tokens, full card details or private chat transcripts.

We may need limited information to verify control of the service identity before deleting it. Automatic identities have no required email address, so we may not be able to identify or recover one after uninstalling or losing the device. A store purchase alone is not proof that you control a particular AI identity. We will explain what we can verify and do; we will not reveal or delete another person's data based on a guessed identifier.

What remains

  • Minimal request, daily usage and cost metadata remains without the deleted identity link for up to 90 days plus the next daily cleanup. It does not contain conversation text or stored API keys.
  • Category-only concern reports remain for up to 90 days plus the next daily cleanup. Deletion removes their service identity link. Reports do not contain your conversation or API key.
  • Limited purchase references and original eligibility dates remain while needed for purchase restoration and enforcing the original included AI period. Deletion does not restart a trial or the Lifetime AI year.
  • Platform device eligibility signals may remain with Apple or Google after uninstalling, resetting the phone or deleting AI data. They do not restore an account, chat or key.
  • Providers may retain requests already sent under their own API policies. Local deletion cannot retract them. Copies you exported, emailed or shared and operating system backups need to be managed separately.

Uninstalling alone does not ask us to delete backend records. If possible, use Delete AI data before uninstalling. Manage subscriptions separately through Google Play or Apple subscriptions. Restore Purchases restores eligible access, not your local chat or API keys.
